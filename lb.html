⚙️ One-Time Setup — Never Needed Again After This

Paste your Google Sheets CSV link once. The leaderboard will remember it, load automatically, and refresh every 30 seconds — no action needed.

  1. Open your Google Form → Responses tab → click the 🟢 Sheets icon to link responses to a Sheet.
  2. Open that Google Sheet → File → Share → Publish to the web
  3. Choose your responses sheet → set format to Comma-separated values (.csv) → click Publish
  4. Copy the URL — it looks like: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/…/pub?output=csv
  5. Your sheet needs one column whose header contains name and one that contains score (case-insensitive, extra words are fine). All other columns from the form are ignored automatically.
